Privacy policy

Aussie Road Rage, by Toybox of JP. Last updated: 16 November 2026

Aussie Road Rage is a small arcade game. It has no accounts and no servers of our own, and we do not sell personal data. This page explains what the game stores and what happens when ads are shown.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, unlocks, trophies and settings (sound, vibration, text size and so on) are saved on your phone only. They are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the game or use Reset in Settings. If you use Android's backup, Google may include this small save file in your device backup under your Google account's own terms.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some rounds, and an optional ad you can watch to earn an extra life. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID and other device and usage information, as described in Google's advertising policies and AdMob's data practices. We do not receive that data and do not see it.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from Credits > Ad choices, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. You can also remove all ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only learns that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off.

Vibration and other permissions

The game asks for permission to use the internet (for ads), to know the network state (for ads) and to vibrate your phone when you are hit. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
