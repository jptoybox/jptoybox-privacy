Privacy policy

Modular Launcher, Retro Line Icon Pack, Retro Colour Icon Pack and Retro Solid Icon Pack. Last updated: 9 November 2026

We do not run any servers, accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not collect or sell personal data. Everything the apps remember is stored on your own phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Modular Launcher

The launcher reads the list of apps installed on your phone only to show them on your home screen and in the app list. Your layout, notes, checklists, snippets and settings are saved on your device only.

Weather card (optional). If you add the Weather card and allow location, the launcher reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open (never in the background). It rounds the position to about 1 km and sends only that position over an encrypted connection to the free MET Norway weather service (api.met.no) to get a forecast. That service's own privacy terms apply to the request (met.no/en/About-us/privacy). The forecast and a place name are kept on your phone and are not sent to us. If you do not add the card or you deny location, no location is read and the launcher makes no network requests. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Agenda card (optional). If you add the Agenda card and allow calendar access, the launcher reads the events already on your phone, from whichever calendar app you use, and shows the next ones on the card. Events are read on your phone only; the launcher does not store them, upload them or share them. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Notification badges (optional). If you turn on badges in the launcher's settings, Android asks you to give the launcher notification access. The launcher then counts how many notifications each app has and shows the number on that app's icon. It does not read, store or send the text, titles or contents of notifications; only the counts are kept, in memory, on your phone. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.

Now playing card (optional). If you add the Now playing card and turn on notification access, the launcher reads the title, artist and play state of whatever media app is playing, and can press play, pause, previous and next for you. This is read on your phone only and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Brightness card (optional). If you add the Brightness card and allow "Modify system settings" for the launcher, moving the slider sets your phone's screen brightness. The launcher reads and changes only the brightness setting. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. You can remove the permission at any time in Android settings.

Photo frame card (optional). If you add the Photo frame card and choose a picture, the launcher uses Android's photo picker, so it never gets access to the rest of your photos. It saves a smaller copy of the one picture you chose in its own private storage on your phone so the card keeps working, and applies the "real photo" or "e-ink colour" look on the phone. The picture is never uploaded or shared, and is deleted when you uninstall the launcher.

Snippets and backups. Snippets you save are stored on your phone. If you use Export in the launcher's settings, a file with your layout, notes, checklists and snippets is saved where you choose; the launcher does not upload it anywhere. The photo for the Photo frame card is not part of the export.

Other permissions. The launcher can ask Android to remove an app (only when you tap Uninstall and confirm) and to open the notification shade (only when you swipe down on the home screen).

Retro Line Icon Pack, Retro Colour Icon Pack and Retro Solid Icon Pack

The icon packs have no network access and ask for no special permissions. They collect nothing. The Retro Line Icon Pack stores your colour choices on your phone and shares them only with Modular Launcher on the same phone.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
